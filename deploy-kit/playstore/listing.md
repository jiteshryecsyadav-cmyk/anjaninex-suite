## Full description
Vyapaar Setu — Indian trading firms (aadhat/agency) ke liye bana poora business app.

🧾 TRADING — Orders, Bills, Goods Return, Payment Receipts, Commission — sab gap-free
numbering aur saaf Hinglish me. Bill photo scan karo, entry khud ban jati hai.

🛍️ BAZAAR LINK — Supplier photo bhejta hai, watermark lag kar sahi buyers tak khud
pahunchti hai. Rate aur quantity ka mol-bhav chat me, agency ki manzoori ke baad
Trading me pakka order apne-aap.

💬 PARTY CHAT — Apni parties se WhatsApp-jaisi chat (photos, reply, zoom) — unhe login
nahi chahiye, mobile OTP se khulti hai. Har party apni saari agencies ek jagah dekhti hai.

📊 SAATH ME — Accounting (CoA, vouchers, P&L), HR & Payroll, Credit score (CREDIL),
Online Dukan, roz ka automatic backup.

Har firm ka data alag-alag aur surakshit (bank-jaisi row-level security). Hinglish me —
jaise mandi me baat hoti hai, waise hi app me.
